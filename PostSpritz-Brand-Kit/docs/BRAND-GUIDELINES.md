# PostSpritz brand guidelines

- Voice: friendly, direct, capable, lightly playful.
- Use the horizontal logo in headers; icon-only mark for app icons and favicons.
- Keep generous clear space around the logo.
- Use orange as an accent with warm white and near-black; avoid overusing orange as a page background.
- The tagline is optional and should be omitted in tight spaces.
- Avoid neon gradients, glow, bevels, stretching, or placing the mark on busy backgrounds.
- Minimum digital width for the horizontal logo: 180px; below that, use the icon-only mark.
